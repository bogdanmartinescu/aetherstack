# Product strategy, adoption and sustainability

## Positioning
Aether UI: one open-code React design system for humans and AI. Compete on complete feature composition, safe source upgrades, deterministic validation, accessible and consistent products and SDK-agnostic AI UI, not raw component count or unsupported "first" claims. shadcn/ui offers registry/MCP/skills; Vercel AI Elements and assistant-ui cover AI interfaces; Mantine/HeroUI/MUI/Untitled UI cover broad or premium components. Validate differentiation through benchmarks, not marketing alone.

Primary users: indie SaaS builders, small product teams, agencies and AI coding agents. Secondary: design-system engineers and organizations with private registries. Focus React, Next, Vite, Tailwind, TypeScript; other frameworks deferred until demand/maintainers justify them.

## Licensing and revenue
Recommended open-core model (owner approval for commercial/legal execution): MIT Core free for personal and commercial use, including existing public components, tokens, themes, public registry, CLI, local MCP, public recipes/skills and local Doctor. Preserve already-granted rights; audit third-party provenance and copyright before legal edits. No mandatory commercial fee, no retroactive relicensing. Sponsors help finance maintenance but are not the sole business model.

GitHub Sponsors/FUNDING.yml only with verified sponsor account and owner permission. Suggested tiers EUR 5/15/49 individual and 199/499/999 corporate, recognition and scoped roadmap discussions, no unlimited support. Accountant/legal review for sponsorship income, VAT, licenses and entity before payments.

Pro hypothesis: SaaS kit EUR 79, AI kit EUR 89 (first two); future CRM/Admin/Commerce/Booking EUR 49–89 if demand. Annual Individual 149, Team (5) 399, Agency (15) 799. Purchased versions remain usable under agreed terms after update subscription expires; no lifetime-update promises. New proprietary materials separate from MIT repository; do not move existing free features behind payment. Enterprise support/integration separately scoped.

Cloud hypothesis: Developer EUR 19/mo, Team 49/mo, Business 149/mo with private registries, shared theme/version history, project/policy reports and hosted team operations. No Cloud build before five credible interested teams, owner approval, budget, secure design and pilot plan; target three paying pilots. Pro build after 15 conversations and owner approval; assess 10 paying customers before more kits. Prices are test hypotheses, no forecast.

## Adoption program
After M0, open preparation for official shadcn registry (verified CLI compatibility prerequisite), official MCP Registry (server.json, npm ownership, publisher validation), Glama, PulseMCP downstream discovery, skills.sh, curated awesome-react/shadcn/MCP lists. Prepare Show HN, DevHunt, Product Hunt, DEV, Reddit communities and React Status/JavaScript Weekly/Frontend Focus pitches. Check current rules and eligibility at time of submission. Use authentic demos rather than spam; owner approves external submissions. Track destination, date, requirements, draft, status, evidence, follow-up.

Docs SEO: AI components/chat, SaaS dashboards, agent UI, MCP, Codex/Claude/Cursor, shadcn interoperability, templates. Each substantive tutorial: runnable source, live example, installation, accessible states, docs and GitHub links. Monthly targets: 4 articles, 4 short demos, 1 substantial tutorial, newsletter outreach and community replies. Use actual reference applications and optional existing-product pilots, never force migration. No fake endorsements.

## Metrics and decision gates
Track successful installs, verified external projects, repeat use, npm trends (not unique people), docs referrals/search, issues, PRs, benchmark outcomes, sponsor revenue, conversion/refunds, churn, support hours. Stretch 90d/6m/12m: stars 100/500/2000; monthly downloads 500/3000/15000; docs visitors 1000/5000/20000; external projects 5/30/100; recipes 10/20/40; benchmark tasks 15/30/50; paying customers 0–5/10–30/75–150. These are goals, not predictions. Prefer retention and real-world use over vanity metrics. Opt-in analytics only for local tooling; aggregate website metrics where appropriate.

## Budget and sustainability
15–20 hours/week, 17h planning baseline (~884h/year, EUR 30,940 opportunity cost at EUR 35/h). Early cash budget EUR 20–235/mo assumption; later EUR 320–1300/mo excluding labor, payment fees, VAT/taxes/refunds/legal. Example gross monthly revenue hypotheses: 10 x 79 + 100 sponsors = 890; 40 x 89 + 400 sponsors + 780 cloud = 4740; 100 x 99 + 1000 sponsors + 3900 cloud = 14800. Product purchases are not MRR. Reconcile net expenses and support time before investing. Organic acquisition initially; paid campaigns require approval and measured conversion.

## Operating cadence
Weekly triage and small releases; monthly metrics, docs, dependency and quality review; quarterly scope/demand decisions. Add features based on recurring pain and benchmark evidence. Risks: saturation, overexpansion, unsafe updates, AI output quality, poor adoption, licensing ambiguity, support burden, cloud cost and burnout. Mitigate with milestone gates, limited framework support, separate licenses, budget caps, tests and feedback.
